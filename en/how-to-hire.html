How to hire

A simple, transparent flow. No scope surprises along the way.

$ steps

01

First contact

You describe the problem (software, automation, data, integration). We reply within 1 business day with framing questions. If it makes sense, we schedule a free 30-minute diagnostic.

02

Diagnostic

A survey of the current environment, risks and goals. It can be remote, with controlled access when needed. The initial conversation lasts about 30 minutes.

03

Proposal

Scope, assumptions, deliverables and work window. You approve before any implementation. The proposal only comes after framing.

04

Execution

Implementation, validation and documentation, with continuous communication on the agreed channel.

05

Handoff

Runbooks, dashboards and transfer to the responsible team. Stabilization support as defined in the proposal.

$ what usually goes into scope

Linux/Docker software development, Kubernetes (k3s), observability stacks (Grafana/Prometheus/Loki/OTel), hardening, basic CI/CD and operations documentation.
